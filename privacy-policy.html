Privacy Policy

Chain Reaction for Android · Last updated 8 August 2026

Short version: the game is playable entirely offline and collects nothing while you just play. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games to post a score, your gamer tag and that score are published on a public leaderboard. That is the only thing that ever leaves your device.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Android application Chain Reaction (package com.mafium.chainreaction), distributed through Google Play. The app is developed and published by an individual developer, contactable at the address at the bottom of this page.

Information collected

Nothing at all, unless you sign in. You can install the game, play every mode, and finish every level without an account, without a network connection, and without anything being collected or transmitted.

Signing in is offered in one place — a "Sign in to post scores" control on the main menu and in the side menu — and exists for one purpose: the Challenge world leaderboard. If you use it, the following is collected:

  • A Google Play Games player identifier and your gamer tag. This is your Play Games display name, not your real name and not your email address. The developer never receives your Google account email through this.
  • An account identifier created by Google Firebase Authentication to represent you.
  • Your best Challenge score and the number of moves it took.
  • The sequence of taps that produced that score. This is recorded so a score can be replayed and checked rather than simply believed. It is stored privately and is never shown to other players — publishing it would hand everyone the solution to a board every player shares.

Your gamer tag, score and move count are public: that is what a leaderboard is. Anyone using the app can see them. The tap log is not.

There is no advertising, no advertising identifier, no analytics, no crash reporting, no location, no contacts, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Permissions requested

The app declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. These exist solely for the leaderboard. No permission that Android considers sensitive — location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage — is requested.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so your progress survives closing it. This never leaves your device except through Android's own backup system, described below. It consists of:

  • Which levels you have unlocked
  • Your best chain score for each level, for Challenge mode, for Zen mode, and for each custom board size you have played
  • Your saved Zen board, so you can pick it up where you left off
  • Your settings: theme, cascade speed, sound on/off, and the cascade rule toggle

The app also writes its sound effects to its own temporary cache folder the first time it runs. These are generated on your device rather than downloaded, and Android may clear them at any time.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Android backup

The saved data above is eligible for Android's standard Auto Backup, so your progress can follow you to a new device. That backup goes to your own Google Drive account under Google's terms, not to the developer, who has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device's system settings under Backup.

Third parties

There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no in-app purchases. The leaderboard is built on two Google services, and they are used only when you sign in:

  • Google Play Games Services — signs you in and supplies your gamer tag.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — stores the leaderboard. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf under the Firebase Privacy and Security terms.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone beyond what is needed to run the leaderboard.

Google Play itself collects information when you download or update any app, independently of this one. That is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping, and deleting, your leaderboard data

A leaderboard entry is kept for as long as it stands, so that records last. You can sign out at any time from the side menu, which stops anything further being posted.

To have your leaderboard entry and its stored tap log deleted, email the address at the bottom of this page from the account you signed in with, or include your gamer tag. It will be removed. Deleting the app alone does not remove an entry that has already been posted, because the leaderboard lives on a server rather than on your phone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13 as a target audience. Nothing is collected from anyone who does not sign in, and signing in requires a Google Play Games profile, which Google governs by its own age rules.

Your rights

If you have never signed in, no data about you exists off your device and there is nothing to access, correct or delete — clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes everything.

If you have signed in, rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to your leaderboard entry and its tap log. Use the contact address below and they will be honoured.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or starts using a network connection, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
